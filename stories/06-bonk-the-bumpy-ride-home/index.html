Bonk! The Bumpy Ride Home

A story about keeping calm, and saying sorry when you make a mistake

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐻 Bear 🐿️ Dr. Squirrel 🦒 Nurse Giraffe
Page 1

After Elephant's operation, the doctors said he could finally go home. Rabbit hurried over to help him up — but she was cross.

"We waited FOREVER for the doctors today! That is NOT okay!" Rabbit huffed, stomping toward the door.

She didn't notice Nurse Giraffe watching from a window nearby — who heard every word, and looked quite hurt.

Page 2

Elephant rested his trunk gently on Rabbit's shoulder. "Hey," he said softly. "I'm okay now. Let's just get home and rest, alright?"

Rabbit took a deep breath. She was still a little grumpy, but Elephant's calm voice helped.

They climbed into their little car, and Rabbit drove them off toward home.

Page 3

They stopped at a red light, waiting patiently.

Behind them, Bear was driving too — but he was busy waving at a friend and wasn't watching the road at all.

BONK! Bear's car bumped right into the back of theirs. Both cars wobbled, and everyone inside got a big, surprising jolt.

Page 4

An ambulance rushed to the scene. Rabbit, Elephant, and Bear were all carefully helped inside and driven straight back to the hospital they had just left.

Nobody was badly hurt — but everyone had a few aches that needed looking at.

Page 5

Bear had hurt his back a little. The doctors gave him some medicine and tucked him into a bed to rest.

Dr. Squirrel remembered what Rabbit had said earlier that day, and felt a bit grumpy too. "Well," she muttered, "let's see how THIS one goes."

But she took a deep breath, and treated Elephant's hurt ankle just as carefully as always.

Page 6

Next it was Rabbit's turn. Her wrist was hurt too.

Even though Dr. Squirrel still felt a little grumpy inside, she was gentle and careful with Rabbit's wrist — just as careful as she was with every single patient.

Page 7

That evening, Dr. Squirrel came to check on them. "Feeling better?" she asked. Elephant nodded happily.

Then she turned to Rabbit. "I'm sorry for how I sounded earlier," she said. "That wasn't fair of me."

"I'm sorry too," Rabbit said quietly. "I shouldn't have shouted at the hospital. You all work so hard." They both smiled — the grumpy feelings melted away.

Page 8

When snack time came, Nurse Giraffe brought Elephant a huge tray — bananas, crackers, and a big glass of juice.

But when she got to Rabbit, she only poured half a glass of juice and walked off without a word.

Nurse Giraffe was still a little upset about that morning, even though she hadn't said so out loud.

Page 9

By dinner time, Nurse Giraffe stopped and thought about it. She marched right back to Rabbit's bed.

"I'm sorry," she said. "I let my feelings get in the way, and that wasn't fair to you." She brought Rabbit a full, delicious dinner — just as good as Elephant's.

From then on, Nurse Giraffe and Dr. Squirrel looked after both of them wonderfully, day after day.

Page 10

After many days of rest and good care, Rabbit's wrist and Elephant's ankle were both fully healed.

Dr. Squirrel and Nurse Giraffe waved them off as they climbed — carefully this time — into their little car and drove home.

Rabbit and Elephant learned something important that day: it's easy to say something grumpy when you're upset. But the bravest thing anyone can do is take a breath, and say sorry once you know you got it wrong. ✨ The End ✨
