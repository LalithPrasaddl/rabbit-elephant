Shh! The Noisy Night

A story about sleepovers, snoring, and saying what you need

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant
Page 1

Rabbit had a BIG idea. "Elephant, come for a sleepover at my burrow!"

Elephant arrived at sunset with his pillow, his teddy, and a suitcase. "What's in the suitcase?" asked Rabbit.
"Snacks," said Elephant. "Just in case."

There was only one small problem. Rabbit's front door was very, very small.

Page 2

Elephant pushed his head in. Then his ears. Then his tummy… and then he STOPPED. His front half was in the burrow, and his back half was in the garden.

"I'm stuck!" he wailed. Rabbit pulled his trunk. Nothing. Rabbit pushed his bottom. Nothing.

So Rabbit tickled his feet. "Hee hee — HOO HOO!" Elephant wiggled… and POP! He zoomed inside like a rocket! WHOOOSH!

Page 3

Elephant flew across the room and landed on Rabbit's sofa. CRUNCH! Now it was a very flat sofa.

Rabbit came tumbling in after him and landed right on top of his head.

"Ta-da!" said Elephant.
"Welcome to my house," said Rabbit, with her ears in a knot.

Page 4

At bedtime, Rabbit showed Elephant the spare bed. It was a lovely bed — for a rabbit.

Elephant lay down. His head fitted. His feet did not. He rolled over and — CRASH! — the bed folded right up, with Elephant squished in the middle! His head stuck out one end and his feet stuck out the other.

So they piled up every cushion in the burrow and made a giant nest on the floor. "Goodnight, Elephant." "Goodnight, Rabbit." Click went the lamp.

Page 5

It was quiet. It was cosy. It was perfect. Then…

HONNNK — shoooo. HONNNK — shoooo.

Elephant was snoring louder than a fire truck! Rabbit's ears blew flat. The pictures on the wall went wibble-wobble. Her blanket blew right off and landed on the lamp.

Rabbit wanted to say something. But Elephant was her guest. It would be rude to wake him, she thought.

Page 6

Rabbit had a sneaky plan. She tiptoed over and clipped a clothes peg onto the end of Elephant's trunk.

The snoring stopped. "Yes!" whispered Rabbit. But Elephant's cheeks puffed up… bigger… and BIGGER… and —

PING! The peg shot off, bounced off the ceiling, bounced off the wall, and BOINK! It landed on Rabbit's nose.
HONNNK — shoooo.

Page 7

Sneaky plan number two: Rabbit would sleep in the kitchen!

She curled up in the big fruit bowl, between a melon and two apples, and her eyes started to close…

Then, in the dark, she heard something creeping closer. Creak. Creak. CREAK.

Page 8

It was Elephant, looking for a midnight snack. He tripped over Rabbit's roller skate, wobbled across the kitchen, and crashed into the shelf. CLANG! A saucepan landed on his head.

Rabbit saw a giant shape with a metal head and a long wiggly trunk. "MONSTER!" she squeaked, and threw an apple. BONK!

"Ow! It's ME!" said a voice from inside the saucepan.

Page 9

Rabbit switched on the light. They stared at each other. Then they both laughed so hard that Rabbit fell out of the fruit bowl.

"Why are you sleeping in the kitchen?" asked Elephant.

Rabbit took a deep breath. "Your snores are very… BIG," she said kindly. "I couldn't sleep."

"Why didn't you tell me?" said Elephant. "My grandma says my snores make the windows rattle. I don't mind at all!"

Page 10

"I know a trick," said Elephant. "When I sleep on my side, my snores get quieter!" Rabbit built a wall of pillows so he couldn't roll back over.

Then Elephant gave one more enormous fake snore — HONNNK! — right in Rabbit's ear. She jumped so high she hit the ceiling.

"Got you!" he giggled. So Rabbit got him back with a pillow. Then he got her back. Soon there were feathers everywhere.

Page 11

At last they snuggled down. Elephant slept on his side, and his snore was soft now — hmm-shoo, hmm-shoo — like a little breeze. Rabbit thought it sounded rather cosy.

In the morning, Elephant woke up first and listened. From under the blanket came a tiny sound: squeak… squeak… squeak.

"Rabbit," he giggled. "YOU snore too!"

When something bothers you, just say so — kindly. Good friends would always rather know. ✨ The End ✨
