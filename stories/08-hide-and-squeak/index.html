Hide and Squeak

A story about losing a game and finding your thing

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🦒 Nurse Giraffe
Page 1

It was a sunny morning in the meadow. "Let's play hide and seek!" said Elephant. "I'm the BEST hider in the whole world."

Nurse Giraffe had the day off. "I'll count," she said, and covered her eyes. "One… two… three…"

Page 2

Rabbit zipped away. Elephant tiptoed behind a tree. It was a very skinny tree. He stood very, very still.

But his ears stuck out on one side. His bottom stuck out on the other side.

"Found you, Elephant!" said Giraffe.
"How?!" said Elephant.

Page 3

Round two. Elephant dived into a bush. Now it looked like a bush with a tail.

Then a bee landed on his nose. Bzzzz. "EEEK!" Elephant jumped out of the bush, ran in a circle, and SPLASH! He sat down in the pond.

Page 4

"Found you again," giggled Giraffe. "But where is Rabbit?"

She looked up high. She looked down low. Then the lid of the picnic teapot lifted up, and out popped Rabbit. "Here I am! I win!"

Page 5

Round three. Elephant hid under the picnic blanket. "I'm a rock," he whispered. "Just a big, quiet rock."

Then the rock went AAAA-CHOO! The blanket flew off and landed on Giraffe's head. She stumbled about, bump, bonk, wobble, and walked right into Elephant. "Found you!"

Page 6

Rabbit won again, curled up inside a flowerpot.

Elephant stamped his foot. THUMP! "This game isn't fair! Rabbit is small and I am BIG! I'm not playing any more!"

The thump was so big that Giraffe's hat bounced off her head.

Page 7

Rabbit's ears drooped. "Okay," she said. "One last round. I'll find a super tricky spot."

She wriggled into a hollow log, deeper and deeper and deeper. Then she tried to wriggle out again… and she couldn't. "Um… help?" she said in a tiny voice.

Page 8

Giraffe looked everywhere, but she couldn't find Rabbit. "Rabbit? Where are you?"

Elephant flapped his big ears and listened hard. Far away there was a little squeak… squeak…

"I can hear her!" he said. "Big ears are very good for listening!"

Page 9

Elephant followed the squeak to the log and poked his trunk inside. Sniff, sniff. It tickled Rabbit's toes. "Hee hee!"

He grabbed her foot and pulled. POP! Rabbit flew out, Elephant fell flat on his bottom, BUMP, and Rabbit landed right on his tummy.

Page 10

"You found me!" cried Rabbit. "You're not the best hider, Elephant. You're the best FINDER!"

Elephant went a little pink. "I'm sorry I got grumpy," he said. "Losing isn't fun. But playing with you is."

Page 11

So they played again, and this time Elephant was the seeker. He found everyone in no time.

Then it was his turn to hide… behind the skinny tree, ears and bottom sticking out. Everybody laughed, and Elephant laughed loudest of all.

Losing a game is okay. Everyone is good at something different, and the best part is playing together. ✨ The End ✨
