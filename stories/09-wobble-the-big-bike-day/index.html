Wobble! The Big Bike Day

A story about falling down and getting back up

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐿️ Dr. Squirrel
Page 1

Rabbit had a shiny new bike. It was red, with a bell that went ring-ring!

There was just one problem. Rabbit didn't know how to ride it.

"Don't worry," said Elephant. "I'll help!"

Page 2

Elephant held the seat. Rabbit pedalled. "Don't let go!" she said. "I won't!" said Elephant.

Then a butterfly landed on his trunk. Elephant let go to look at it, and Rabbit went wibble… wobble… CRASH! She rolled straight into a hedge. Only her feet stuck out.

Page 3

Second try. Rabbit pedalled faster and faster. "I'm doing it!"

But she couldn't steer. She zoomed down the path and straight into a big muddy puddle. SPLOSH! Now she was a brown rabbit with mud on her ears.

Page 4

"Let me show you how," said Elephant. He sat down on the tiny bike.

CRUNCH! The bike went flat as a pancake. The bell said ding… dunk in a very sad voice.

Page 5

Just then Dr. Squirrel came by with her doctor's bag and her toolbox. She put a plaster on Rabbit's knee, fixed the bike, and added two little wheels at the back.

"Training wheels," she said. "They help you balance."

Page 6

Now Rabbit could ride! Round and round the meadow she went. Then she went down a hill. Bumpety, bumpety, BUMP! PING! PING! The training wheels flew off.

One bounced across the grass and BONK! landed on Elephant's head like a hat.

Page 7

Rabbit tumbled into the long grass. She threw down her helmet. "I'm no good at this," she sniffed. "I'll never, ever learn."

Page 8

Elephant sat down beside her. "When I learned to roller skate," he said, "I fell over a hundred times. I fell on my bottom. I fell on my nose. Once I rolled right into the duck pond, and a duck sat on my head."

Rabbit giggled. She couldn't help it.

Page 9

"Every fall means you're learning," said Elephant. "Let's try one more time."

Rabbit put her helmet back on. Elephant held the seat and ran beside her, puffing and panting. "Don't let go!" "I won't!"

Page 10

Rabbit pedalled and pedalled. The wind whooshed past her ears. "Elephant, am I doing it?"

She looked back. Elephant was far, far behind, waving his trunk. He had let go ages ago!

"I'M DOING IT!" Rabbit shouted. Ring-ring!

Page 11

Now Rabbit rides everywhere, and Elephant wants to learn too. Dr. Squirrel found him the biggest bike in the meadow.

He wobbled… and wobbled… and CRASH! He landed in the hedge, and the whole hedge fell over.

"Don't worry!" called Rabbit. "Every fall means you're learning!"

Everybody falls when they learn something new. Get back up and try again! ✨ The End ✨
