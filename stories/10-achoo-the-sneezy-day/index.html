Achoo! The Sneezy Day

A story about sneezing into your elbow

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐑 Dr. Sheep
Page 1

Elephant woke up with a tickly trunk. Sniff, sniff. "I'm fine!" he said. "Let's go hang up the washing, Rabbit."

Rabbit put on her favourite straw hat, and off they went.

Page 2

Elephant's trunk began to wiggle. "Ah… ah… ah… ACHOO!"

Rabbit's hat flew off her head, spun round and round, and landed at the very top of a tree. "My hat!" said Rabbit.

Page 3

They hung up the washing: socks, shirts and one big bedsheet. "Ah… ah… ACHOO!"

Everything flew off the line. Two socks landed on Rabbit's ears, and the sheet landed on Elephant. "Whooo! I'm a ghost!" he said.

Page 4

"You need to see Dr. Sheep," said Rabbit.

On the way, Elephant's trunk wiggled again. "ACHOO!" Rabbit tumbled head over heels, right into a bush. Boing! Only her feet stuck out.

Page 5

Dr. Sheep listened to Elephant's chest. "Say ahhh," she said.

"Ah… ah… ACHOO!" Dr. Sheep's wool blew up big and fluffy, like a giant cloud. Her glasses ended up on her ear.

Page 6

Dr. Sheep fixed her glasses. "You have a cold," she said. "When you sneeze, tiny germs fly out. They're too small to see, but they land on everything, and they can make your friends sick too."

Elephant looked at Rabbit. "Oh no!"

Page 7

"So when a sneeze comes," said Dr. Sheep, "sneeze into your elbow, like this."

Elephant tried. But his trunk was so long that it poked out the other side. "Fold it up!" said Rabbit. Elephant tucked his trunk away. Perfect!

Page 8

"And then wash your hands with soap," said Dr. Sheep. "Scrub while you sing a song."

Elephant scrubbed and sang and sprayed water with his trunk. Soon there were bubbles on everybody's heads.

Page 9

Back home, Rabbit tucked Elephant into bed with a bowl of warm soup.

Then his trunk wiggled. "Ah… ah…" Rabbit dived behind the sofa and held on tight.

Page 10

Elephant tucked his trunk into his elbow. "…achoo."

It was the tiniest sneeze ever. Nothing flew anywhere at all. Rabbit peeked out. "Was that it?" They laughed and laughed.

Page 11

A few days later Elephant felt all better. They hung up the washing again, and Giraffe got Rabbit's hat down from the tree.

When you sneeze, use your elbow, and wash your hands. That's how you keep your friends safe. ✨ The End ✨
