The Great Big Cake

A story about taking one step at a time

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐻 Bear
Page 1

Tomorrow was Bear's birthday. "Let's bake him a great big cake!" said Rabbit, holding up a recipe. "First we measure the flour."

Elephant yawned. "Recipes are so slow. Let's be quick!"

Page 2

Elephant didn't measure. He tipped in the whole bag. POOF! A big white cloud filled the kitchen.

When it cleared, Rabbit and Elephant were white from head to toe. "Boo!" said Elephant. "We're snowmen!"

Page 3

"Next, crack two eggs," read Rabbit.

Elephant was in a hurry, so he cracked them with a big BONK. The shells went in the bowl, and the eggs went on Rabbit's head. Drip, drip.

Page 4

"Now mix it slowly," read Rabbit. Elephant turned the mixer to SUPER FAST. WHIRRRR!

Cake mix splatted on the walls and the ceiling, and one big blob dripped onto Elephant's nose. Splot.

Page 5

The cake went in the oven. "Now we wait thirty minutes," said Rabbit. But Elephant couldn't wait. He opened the oven to peek. Peek… peek… PEEK!

The cake had been growing big and tall. Then it went pfffff and turned as flat as a pancake.

Page 6

Ding-dong! It was Bear, a day early! "Quick, hide everything!"

Elephant sat on the flat cake. Rabbit stood in front of the mess. Bear blinked. "Why are you two covered in snow?"

Page 7

Rabbit's ears drooped. "We were making you a birthday cake. But we rushed, and it all went wrong."

Bear smiled. "Baking is like climbing stairs," he said. "One step at a time. Let's try again together."

Page 8

This time Rabbit read each step out loud. Elephant scooped exactly one cup of flour with his trunk, very carefully.

Bear cracked the eggs gently. Tap, tap. Not one bit of shell went in.

Page 9

Then came the hardest step: waiting. Elephant's trunk kept creeping towards the oven door. Rabbit held on to it.

They sang songs and counted to a hundred, and they didn't peek once.

Page 10

DING! Out came a big, tall, fluffy cake. "Hooray!"

Elephant jumped up to cheer, and Bear started to laugh. The flat cake was still stuck to Elephant's bottom!

Page 11

They ate the tall cake and the flat "pancake cake" too, and Bear said both were delicious.

When we rush, things get messy. When we go one step at a time and wait patiently, things turn out just right. ✨ The End ✨
