Whoosh! The Kite Day

A story about taking turns

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🦒 Nurse Giraffe
Page 1

It was a windy day. Nurse Giraffe brought a present: a bright red kite with a long, wiggly tail.

"It's for both of you," she said. "So you'll have to share."

Page 2

"Me first!" said Rabbit, and grabbed the string. "No, me first!" said Elephant, and grabbed it too.

Tug! Tug! Tug! The kite zigzagged up and down, round and round.

Page 3

Elephant gave one great big pull, and Rabbit let go. Elephant fell backwards. BUMP!

The kite dived down and bonked Giraffe right on the nose. "Ow!"

Page 4

Rabbit grabbed the string and ran. Then a huge gust of wind came. WHOOOSH!

The kite pulled up, and so did Rabbit! Her feet left the ground, and she floated up like a balloon. "Heeelp!"

Page 5

Elephant ran after her and caught her feet with his trunk. But the wind pulled Elephant too, sliding him through the mud.

Giraffe grabbed his tail. Now they were all holding on in a long, wobbly line!

Page 6

The wind stopped. Rabbit dropped down into Elephant's arms. Plop.

But the kite flew away, all by itself, and got stuck at the very top of a tall tree.

Page 7

"It was YOUR fault!" said Rabbit. "No, YOUR fault!" said Elephant.

Giraffe shook her head. "When two friends pull on one string," she said, "nobody gets to fly the kite."

Page 8

Then Giraffe stretched her long, long neck up to the top of the tree and got the kite back.

She also got a bird's nest stuck on her head. "Tweet!" said the bird.

Page 9

"Let's take turns," said Rabbit. "You go first, Elephant."

Rabbit held the kite up high and ran, and the kite soared up. Elephant flew it while Rabbit counted slowly to fifty.

Page 10

Then they swapped. Elephant held on to Rabbit's belt so she wouldn't float away again. The kite flew higher and higher.

Rabbit found out that watching a friend have fun was fun too.

Page 11

Last of all it was Giraffe's turn. With her long neck, the kite went higher than the clouds. "Wheee!" everyone cheered.

When we take turns, everybody gets to play, and nobody ends up in a tree. ✨ The End ✨
