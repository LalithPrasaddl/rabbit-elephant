Grow, Seed, Grow!

A story about waiting for good things

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐿️ Dr. Squirrel
Page 1

Rabbit found a seed on the path. It was big and stripy, with a pointy end.

"Let's plant it!" said Elephant. Dig, plop, pat! They tucked the seed into the ground.

Page 2

Five minutes later, Rabbit put her ear to the ground. "Is it growing yet?"

Nothing. Not one leaf. Elephant tapped his big foot. "Hurry UP, seed!"

Page 3

"It must be thirsty!" said Elephant. He filled his trunk at the pond and… SPLOOSH!

Water everywhere! Rabbit was soaked, and the garden turned into one big muddy puddle.

Page 4

"Maybe it's stuck," said Rabbit, and she dug the seed up to have a look.

It was still just a seed: a very wet, very muddy seed. "It hasn't done ANYTHING!" said Elephant.

Page 5

Just then, Dr. Squirrel came by on her morning walk. "Goodness!" she said. "What are you doing to that poor seed?"

"Making it grow," said Rabbit. "FAST," said Elephant.

Page 6

"A seed needs three things," said Dr. Squirrel. "Soil to snuggle in. A little drink of water. And warm sunshine."

"That's easy!" said Rabbit. "And one more thing," said Dr. Squirrel. "Time."

Page 7

"Under the ground, where you can't see, a seed is very busy," she said. "First a root wiggles down into the dark. Then a little shoot reaches up for the light."

"So it IS working," said Rabbit. "It just needs us to leave it alone."

Page 8

So they planted the seed again, gently. Elephant gave it one tiny sprinkle. Not a SPLOOSH, just a sprinkle.

Rabbit whispered, "Goodnight, little seed. Take your time."

Page 9

They waited. It rained, and then the sun came out. They played, and slept, and waited some more.

Then one morning… "LOOK!" A tiny green shoot was poking up out of the soil!

Page 10

The shoot grew and grew. Soon it was taller than Rabbit. Then it was taller than Elephant!

"How did it get so BIG?" said Elephant. "Soil, water, sunshine," said Rabbit, "and time."

Page 11

At last a great big sunflower opened, as bright as the sun, and full of brand new stripy seeds.

"Now we have LOTS to plant," said Elephant. And they both knew the secret: good things take time to grow. ✨ The End ✨
