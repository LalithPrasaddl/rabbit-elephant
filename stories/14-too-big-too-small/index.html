Too Big, Too Small

A story about how everybody fits somewhere

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐻 Bear
Page 1

It was a sunny day at the playground. Elephant climbed up the slide, and up, and up, and sat down at the top…

…and got stuck. "Uh-oh," said Elephant. "I'm too BIG!"

Page 2

Then they tried the seesaw. Elephant sat down on his end. BOING!

Rabbit shot up into the air on the other end, and stayed there. "Get me DOWN!" she squeaked.

Page 3

Rabbit scampered up the ladder into the treehouse. Elephant tried to follow, but only his trunk fit through the door.

He sighed. "I'm too big for EVERYTHING."

Page 4

Next door, Bear was standing under his apple tree. "I'm making apple crumble," he said, "but the apples are too high."

Rabbit jumped and jumped. She stood on her tippy-tippy-toes. "I'm too SMALL!"

Page 5

So Rabbit piled up a bucket, a box and a flowerpot, and climbed right to the top. Wibble… wobble…

CRASH! Down she came, with the flowerpot on her head.

Page 6

Rabbit and Elephant sat under the tree, feeling glum. "I'm too big," said Elephant. "I'm too small," said Rabbit.

Bear scratched his head. "Hmm," he said. "Too big AND too small. That gives me an idea…"

Page 7

But Elephant had the same idea first. "Hop on!" he said. He lifted Rabbit with his trunk, higher and higher, right up into the branches.

"I can reach!" cried Rabbit.

Page 8

Rabbit sat on Elephant's head and picked the apples, and Elephant caught them in Bear's basket.

"One, two, three…" they counted, all the way up to nine. "Just one more!"

Page 9

But apple number ten rolled away and plopped into a little hole in a log. Elephant's trunk was much too big to fit.

"Leave it to me!" said Rabbit. She wriggled right in. "Got it!"

Page 10

Bear made the most delicious apple crumble ever. "I couldn't have done it without you," he said.

"Elephant was big enough to reach up high, and Rabbit was small enough to squeeze in tight."

Page 11

Back at the playground, Elephant sat down and stretched his trunk out long. Rabbit climbed up onto his head, and slid all the way down. "Wheee! You're the best slide EVER!"

Big or small, everybody fits somewhere. ✨ The End ✨
