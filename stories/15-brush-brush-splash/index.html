Brush, Brush, Splash!

A story about brushing your teeth

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐑 Dr. Sheep
Page 1

It was nearly bedtime. Rabbit picked up her toothbrush. "Time to brush our teeth!"

Elephant blinked. "Brush my WHAT?" He had never brushed his teeth before. Not even once.

Page 2

So Rabbit lent him her toothbrush. Elephant held it in his trunk and opened his mouth wide… very, VERY wide.

Scrub, scrub, scrub… One tooth done. "This is going to take ALL NIGHT!"

Page 3

Then Elephant had a big idea. He fetched the broom from the kitchen. SCRITCH! SCRATCH!

Bristles flew everywhere. "Ptooey!" said Elephant. "Brooms are for floors, not for teeth!"

Page 4

"I need more toothpaste," said Elephant, and he stepped on the tube with his great big foot. SPLURT!

Toothpaste shot across the bathroom, all over Rabbit. "I look like a snowman!" she spluttered.

Page 5

Next, Elephant filled his trunk at the tap to rinse. He sucked up ALL the water in the sink… SPLAAASH!

Water everywhere! Rabbit's rubber duck went floating right out of the door.

Page 6

Knock, knock! It was Dr. Sheep from next door, in her dressing gown. "What is all this splashing?"

"I'm brushing my teeth," said Elephant, dripping. Dr. Sheep tried very hard not to laugh.

Page 7

Dr. Sheep opened her doctor's bag. Inside was a brand-new toothbrush, just the right size for an elephant.

"And you only need a tiny blob of toothpaste," she said. "No bigger than a pea."

Page 8

"Brush gently, round and round," said Dr. Sheep. "The front teeth, the back teeth, the top teeth and the bottom teeth."

"And your tongue!" giggled Rabbit. "It tickles!"

Page 9

Dr. Sheep turned over a little sand timer. "Keep brushing until all the sand has run down. That's two whole minutes."

Brush, brush, brush… Elephant hummed. Rabbit hummed. The sand trickled down.

Page 10

"Now spit, ptoo! And then a LITTLE sip of water," said Dr. Sheep. "A little sip, Elephant."

Elephant took the littlest sip ever. Then he smiled in the mirror. Sparkle!

Page 11

Soon they were snuggled up in bed. In the dark, Elephant gave a great big grin.

"Look, Rabbit! My teeth are so shiny, they're like a night-light!" Clean teeth, sweet dreams. ✨ The End ✨
