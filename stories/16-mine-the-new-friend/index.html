Mine! The New Friend

A story about making room for one more

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐢 Little Tortoise
Page 1

One morning, someone new moved in next door. It was Little Tortoise, come to stay with his grandpa.

"Hello!" said Elephant. "Wow, you brought your house with you!" Little Tortoise giggled. "It's my shell, silly!"

Page 2

Little Tortoise could do a brilliant trick. He tucked in his head and his legs, and spun round and round like a spinning top.

Elephant laughed and laughed. But Rabbit folded her arms. "Elephant is MY friend," she grumbled. "MINE!"

Page 3

"Watch me, Elephant!" shouted Rabbit. She did a cartwheel. She did another cartwheel… CRASH! Right into a bush.

But Elephant was too busy cheering for Little Tortoise to notice.

Page 4

So Rabbit grabbed a pot and a spoon and made the LOUDEST band in the whole meadow. BANG! CLANG! BONG!

"Too LOUD!" cried Elephant, and his big ears flopped right down over his eyes.

Page 5

Then Rabbit tried her biggest trick of all. She climbed on top of a big beach ball. "Look at ME!"

Wibble… wobble… The ball rolled down the hill, faster and faster… SPLOSH! Right into the pond.

Page 6

"Help!" squeaked Rabbit. Little Tortoise got there first. "Hold on to my shell!" he called.

He paddled her all the way back to the shore, slow and steady, like a little green boat.

Page 7

Rabbit sat on the grass, dripping. "I'm sorry," she sniffed. "I just wanted you to play with ME."

Elephant gave her a big, gentle hug. "Having a new friend doesn't mean I like you any less. My heart is big, like me. There's room for everybody!"

Page 8

Rabbit turned to Little Tortoise. "Thank you for saving me. And I'm sorry I was grumpy."

Little Tortoise smiled. "That's okay! Do you want to play with my skipping rope? It needs THREE."

Page 9

Elephant held one end of the rope in his trunk, and Little Tortoise held the other. Round and round it went, slow and steady.

Rabbit jumped and jumped. "One, two, three, four, FIVE!"

Page 10

Then it was Elephant's turn to jump. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

The whole meadow bounced, and Rabbit and Little Tortoise bounced right up into the air with it!

Page 11

At sunset, the three friends sat on the hill and shared a big bowl of strawberries.

"Two friends are good," said Rabbit, "but three friends are even better!" There's always room for one more friend. ✨ The End ✨
