Thank You, Dr. Squirrel!

A story about saying thank you

🐰 Rabbit 🐘 Elephant 🐿️ Dr. Squirrel 🦒 Nurse Giraffe 🐑 Dr. Sheep
Page 1

Rabbit and Elephant popped into the clinic to say hello. But everybody looked SO tired.

Dr. Squirrel yawned. Nurse Giraffe's long neck drooped. Dr. Sheep flopped into a chair. "What a busy, busy day," she sighed.

Page 2

On the way home, Elephant was thinking. "Dr. Squirrel took the thorn out of my foot," he said. "And she put a plaster on your knee."

"They help everybody," said Rabbit. "But who helps THEM?" Then she had an idea. "Let's say THANK YOU, with a surprise party!"

Page 3

First they painted a great big banner. Elephant dipped his trunk in the paint and wrote THANK YOU in giant wiggly letters.

Then he swished his trunk… SPLAT! Now Rabbit had purple spots all over!

Page 4

Next they made everybody's favourite food: acorn pudding for Dr. Squirrel, clover tea for Dr. Sheep, and a big leafy salad for Nurse Giraffe.

Elephant carried the whole wobbly pile on his head, very, very carefully.

Page 5

They crept into the clinic waiting room while the doctors were busy. "Shh!" whispered Rabbit.

Elephant tried to tiptoe too. Thump… thump… BUMP! He knocked the coat stand, and a white coat landed right on his head.

Page 6

Rabbit hung up the banner, and Elephant blew up the balloons with his trunk. He blew… and blew… and blew… BANG!

"What was THAT?" called Dr. Squirrel. "Quick!" whispered Rabbit. "Hide!"

Page 7

The door opened, and in came Dr. Squirrel, Nurse Giraffe and Dr. Sheep. "SURPRISE!" shouted Rabbit and Elephant, jumping out.

The doctors were so surprised that Nurse Giraffe's cap popped right off!

Page 8

"Thank you for taking out my thorn," said Elephant. "Thank you for fixing my knee," said Rabbit.

"Thank you for looking after EVERYBODY!" they said together. Dr. Squirrel wiped away a happy tear.

Page 9

Then more friends came to the party. Pig brought flowers, Fox brought a card, and Lion gave a great big thank-you ROAR!

Everybody had someone to thank.

Page 10

Everyone tucked into their favourite food. "Do you know," said Dr. Squirrel, munching her acorn pudding, "a thank you is the best medicine of all."

"I don't feel tired one bit!" said Nurse Giraffe.

Page 11

Walking home under the evening sky, Rabbit skipped. "Saying thank you made ME feel happy too!"

"A thank you is a little present," said Elephant, "and you can give one to anybody, any day." ✨ The End ✨
